How the file should be formatted
The parser (parse_docx_questions) recognises:
• Questions: lines starting with 1., 2) etc.
• Options: A., B), C., D. on separate lines
• Answers: a line like Answer: b (also accepts Ans: c, Correct answer: A)
• Marks: (2 marks) or (2 mks) in the question line
• Images & tables: kept, images saved to static/question_images/
